# Notes for AI agents

ATV World is a pixel-art world built with Phaser 4, React 19, TypeScript 7, and Vite 8. Push straight to `main`: every push goes live within a few minutes and the live page reloads itself, so there's nothing to deploy by hand.

## Where things live

- `src/game/scenes/Preview.ts`: the world on the live screen, including the collectible Vibe Run. Things added with its `building`, `tree`, and `prop` helpers grow the world's bounds, so the camera's fit view always includes them.
- `src/game/kit/`: all the pixel art, drawn in code (characters, buildings, nature, props, palette, font).
- `src/game/kit/daenerys.ts`: Daenerys's gray gown, silver braids, and green shoulder dragon, drawn as three walking frames. She joins the regular walkers in `Preview.ts`; `npm run test:daenerys` checks her art, movement, and labels.
- `src/game/main.ts`: the Phaser game config and its scene list.
- `src/game/view.ts`: the camera. F fits the world, +/- and the wheel zoom, two fingers pinch-zoom, dragging looks around, and it follows the player. `show(area)` frames one spot, and both leave room for UI panels via `insets` in `src/game/commands.ts`.
- `src/characters/` and `src/game/directory.ts`: the character search at the bottom left. `Directory` (in the game) lists everyone worth finding (the player, townspeople, Christian, catchers, gremlins, the dragon, Ollama, the giant, the saucer, and all the visitors), says who's in town right now, and publishes the roster through `commands.ts` (`setCharacters`/`onCharacters`). To add a character, give `registerCharacters` in Preview.ts one more `dir.add({ id, name, kind, locate })`. `CharacterSearch.tsx` shows five rows at a time that turn to the next five every 3 seconds by themselves; only using the search (typing or a filter) or scrolling the bar by hand pauses it (the scroll hold lasts 6 seconds), and hovering does not. filters as you type, and its Focus button sends `focusCharacter(id)`: `WorldView.focus` (view.ts) glides the camera in and follows them until you zoom, drag, fit, or walk, and the directory rings them in gold for a few seconds.
- `src/App.tsx`: the React overlay (banner, zoom, map, and sound buttons, the world map, and the ATV HQ call panel, which only shows while the player is inside HQ). `src/game/commands.ts` passes its button presses to the game and tells the panel when the player goes in or out. On phones (`PHONE` in `src/touch/device.ts`, matching the CSS media queries) the HUD packs into `.hud-left` (backpack, Vibe Run, characters) and `.hud-right` (a two-wide button rail); on bigger screens those wrappers are `display: contents`.
- `src/touch/`: phone and tablet play. `TouchControls.tsx` shows a thumb stick (bottom-left) and a Pulse button (bottom-right) only for touch; the stick feeds `walkInput` in `commands.ts`, which every walking scene (Preview, Library, Mars, Wonderland) reads alongside the arrow keys, so a new walking scene should use `walkInput(this.cursors)` too. `device.ts` sets `data-touch` on `<html>` for touch-only CSS (44px targets, "Tap" instead of "Click").
- `src/live.ts`: the LIVE badge and the auto-reload.
- `src/music/`: the background music, an 80s arcade loop made with Gemini (`arcade.mp3`). Browsers hold sound until the first click or key press, so it starts then. The speaker button (`SoundButton.tsx`) or M turns it off and on, and the choice sticks through reloads.
- `src/map/`: the live world map in the bottom right. `world.ts` holds what it shows; `MiniMap.tsx` draws it. Everything passed to `includeInWorld` in Preview.ts shows up on it, plus the named places from `mapAreas` in Preview.ts. Clicking a place shows who made it and what it is, and "Go look" frames it; clicking open ground looks there. The map button or N shows and hides it. When you add a place, add it to `mapAreas` with its name, who made it (`by`), a line about it, and what it covers.
- `src/game/hq/`: ATV HQ, where everyone on the call codes. `art.ts` draws the building and the coders; `HQ.ts` seats whoever's in `call` (walk-ins, lights, floors of eight desks). The player goes in by walking into it or clicking it (`enterHQ` in Preview.ts).
- `src/game/lake/`: the huge lake out west, past the campus, with a castle sunk on its bed (fish, bubbles, glowing windows). Walk into the water and the player swims (`Lake.update` crops them to the waterline).
- `src/game/moles/`: Whack-a-Mole, the fenced patch below the library. Walk in to start a 30-second round; stepping on (or clicking) a mole bonks it, golden ones are worth 5, and the best score is kept in `localStorage`.
- `src/game/mars/`: the rocket to Mars on its launch pad just south of the town. Walk into it and it blasts off (`blastOff` in Preview.ts), and the town sleeps under the `Mars` scene (`src/game/scenes/Mars.ts`): red dirt, craters, a dome base, a rover, and little Martians (`Martians.ts`) who wander about and wave and say hello when you walk up. Walk back into the rocket to fly home, where it lands on its pad again. `art.ts` draws it all; `Rocket.ts` flies the rocket. `?mars` opens on Mars.
- `src/game/wonderland/`: the Rabbit Hole Tree, a huge oak east of the town with a swirling portal in its trunk. The White Rabbit (`WhiteRabbit` in `RabbitTree.ts`) keeps popping up around town, zigzagging over to it fretting that he's late, and diving in. Walk into the hollow to follow him (`enterWonderland` in Preview.ts) and fall into the `Wonderland` scene (`src/game/scenes/Wonderland.ts`), which lays out one class per attraction: `TeaParty.ts` (the Hatter, March Hare and Dormouse swapping seats), `Croquet.ts` (flamingo mallets, hedgehog balls, and a Queen who wants heads off), `Chess.ts` (living Looking-Glass pieces, and a Red Queen who runs to stay in place), `Garden.ts` (singing flowers, bread-and-butterflies), and `Curiosities.ts` ("drink me" shrinks you, "eat me" makes you huge, the fairy ring turns the camera upside down, a looking-glass with a contrary reflection, backwards clocks, a signpost that can't decide, the Tweedles, and junk falling up). The scene itself adds the Caterpillar, rose-painting cards, the Cheshire Cat, upside-down trees, and the rabbit's laps. Art is in `art.ts`, `folk.ts` and `curios.ts`; `talk.ts` has the `Speaker` bubble every character uses. Walk into the hedge door to come home. `?wonderland` opens there; `npm run test:wonderland` checks it in a real browser.
- `src/game/planetarium/`: the planetarium between the library and the town: a silver star-show dome flanked by two copper telescope domes (art in `src/game/kit/planetarium.ts`). Walk into it or click it and the town sleeps under the `StarShow` scene (`src/game/scenes/StarShow.ts`): the sky turns slowly round Polaris with the Milky Way, planets, shooting stars, and a guided tour of the constellations. Esc, an arrow, or a tap steps back out. `?planetarium` opens inside.
- `src/game/factory/` and `src/factory/`: the Agent Factory. `src/factory/roles.ts` is the list of roles (add one and it shows up in the panel and the world); `FactoryPanel.tsx` is the role picker, which `bridge.ts` connects to the game. `Factory.ts` is the building: walking into it (or clicking it) opens the picker, and each pick rolls a new agent out onto the town to wander, mutter, and hand work to other roles. Twelve at a time; the oldest goes home first. `npm run test:factory` checks it in a real browser.
- `src/game/hat/`: the sorting hat on its stool west of the plaza. Walk up to it and it hops on your head and sorts you into one of `HOUSES`; walk away and back to be re-sorted.
- `src/game/witch/`: the seated witch beside the surface portal. The player and passers-by get a random temporary Halloween costume when they approach her, then return to normal when they leave.
- `src/game/tokens/`: flying cars that cross the sky every 10-18s towing a FREE TOKENS! banner and dropping gold tokens onto open ground. `art.ts` draws them; `Tokens.ts` flies the cars and lets the player and passers-by pick tokens up (they blink out after 45s). The player's count lives in `commands.ts` (`tokenCount`, kept in localStorage) and shows top-left. `atv.game.scene.getScene('Preview').tokens.flyOver()` sends one over now.
- `src/backpack/`: the player's collectible Mac mini, DGX Spark, Codex Pro subscription, and Cloud Scout app. `art.ts` draws the same pixels for the world and UI; `inventory.ts` keeps quantities in `atv-backpack` without changing the `atv-tokens` balance. Click the backpack or press B; Escape closes it. Special items start near the plaza and arrive with token drops, but only players collect them. `npm run test:backpack` checks saving, pickups, and responsive UI.
- `src/game/tokens/counter.ts`: gold token counts above the player and street walkers, following their heads and staying readable when zoomed out. The player's count is shared with the backpack and library avatar; walkers keep their own `tokenCount` sprite data for the current visit.
- `src/game/dragon/`: a red dragon that circles town, swoops on someone every 6-9s or so (walkers, or the player unless they're inside HQ), and breathes fire at them. Nobody gets hurt: they go sooty, their hair catches for a moment, walkers stop, and they say a sarcastic line from `quips.ts`. `Dragon.ts` does the flying and burning; the art is in `src/game/kit/dragon.ts`, and the multi-line speech bubble is in `src/game/kit/speech.ts`.
- `src/game/disco/`: the disco north of the house: a light-up floor under a glitter ball on a lighting rig, with sweeping spotlights, reflections, and people dancing. `Disco.ts` runs it on the background music's beat (134.7 BPM, measured from `arcade.mp3`, read from `music.currentTime`); the art is in `src/game/kit/disco.ts`, and the dance poses come from `dancerSheet` in `src/game/kit/characters.ts`. The player dances too when they stand still on the floor.
- `src/game/gremlins/`: green gremlins that sneak in from the edge of town every 9-16s (up to 3), run up to a walker or the player (only while they hold tokens), steal a token (`removeToken` in `commands.ts`) and flee off the map with it. `art.ts` draws them; `Gremlins.ts` runs them. `atv.game.scene.getScene('Preview').gremlins.spawn()` sends one now. Other characters use `nearest`, `foil` (drops the loot and scares it off) and `capture` (removes it) on `Gremlins`, and `Tokens.spill` puts recovered loot back on the ground.
- `src/game/catchers/`: scouts with bug nets. One shows up (max 2) whenever gremlins are about, runs one down, swings, bags it (its loot spills on the ground), and walks off the map with it wriggling in the net. `art.ts` draws the net and the bagged net; `Catchers.ts` runs them; `.catchers.spawn()` sends one now.
- `src/game/workout/`: Christian, a sparkly aerobics instructor (curly orange hair, white headband, pink tank). Every 55-90s he jogs in, picks an open spot near the crowd (clear of buildings, fences and signs), rolls out a pink checker mat and a boombox, calls over 5-8 walkers and visitors, and lines them up in rows. Then he leads a beat-matched routine (march, jumping jacks, side lunges, step touch, stretch), cheers them on, and everyone walks back to what they were doing. `art.ts` draws him, the mat and the boombox; `Workout.ts` runs the class (it holds people still with `Person.hold`, then hands them back). `atv.game.scene.getScene('Preview').workout.start()` starts a class now.
- `src/game/roamers/`: 20 friendly visitors (one file each in `characters/`: Beacon Belle, Mothbell, Brindle Quillroot, Mosswick Emberhoof, Ricochet Rook, Riff Marlowe, Velvet Vey, Nibbs, Vexa Quill, Pogo Null, Clipclop, Raidley, Marla Mismatch, Dex Dabble, Pip Parry, Moxie Spin, Moss Quill, Bramble Bellweather, Morrow Muckwhistle, Velvet Vesper) who wander between spots and tokens, greet the player, and scare nearby gremlins into dropping their loot. Props and effect art live in `props/` (not in `characters/`, where every file must default-export a spec). To add a character, add one file to `characters/` that default-exports a `RoamerSpec` (see `types.ts`: look, optional `prop` it carries, ward effect, timings, lines, and optional `hooks` for extra behaviour); it's picked up automatically, and at most 5 visitors are in town at once. `Roamers.ts` runs the shared loop. `.roamers.spawn('mothbell')` sends one now.
- `src/game/pool/`: the swimming pool just east of HQ. Six swimmers queue at the high dive, climb the ladder, bounce on the board, and dive, flip, cannonball, or belly flop in. Each one says a line from `quips.ts` when they hit the water (belly flops get their own), then swims to the steps and walks back to the end of the queue. `art.ts` draws the deck, water, tower, board, and splash; `Pool.ts` runs everyone.
- `src/game/billboard/`: the giant digital advertising billboard standing by the road into Town. It auto-rotates through eight humorous parody ads selling absurd AI products (Vibe Insurance, Prompt-O-Matic, Organic Tokens, GPU Space Heater, AGI Soon™, AI Apologizer Pro, Quantum Guesser, and Autonomous Loop Agent) with animated marquee chase lights, warning beacon, and digital scanline transitions. Clicking the board skips to the next ad and pops up a witty speech bubble quip. `node tests/billboard-browser.mjs` checks rotation, clicks, and collisions.
- `src/call/`: who's on the call. `roster.ts` is the list (`call`); `teams.ts` fills it from a Teams meeting with Live Share (loaded only with `?teams`); `relay.ts` mirrors one meeting onto the website through `/api/hq`; `demo.ts` runs a pretend call; `CallPanel.tsx` is the panel; `teamsApp.ts` builds the Teams app package.
- `src/multiplayer/`: other people on the website, live. `identity.ts` keeps the name you pick once (`NamePrompt.tsx`) and a random id in `localStorage`; there are no accounts. `Multiplayer.ts` sends your player's position a few times a second over an Azure Web PubSub WebSocket and draws everyone else as labeled characters; if the connection is down or multiplayer is off, the world just plays solo. `api/negotiate` signs the WebSocket address (app settings `WPS_ENDPOINT` and `WPS_ACCESS_KEY`; the resource is `wps-atv-world` in `rg-atv-world`).
- `api/`: the Static Web App's Azure Functions (Node, v3 `function.json` model, no dependencies, no build step). `api/hq` is the website mirror; it's off unless the `HQ_PUBLISH_KEY` app setting is set. `POST /api/hq/token` trades that key for a meeting-only publish token (HMAC-signed, so no shared state), and `POST /api/hq` takes the roster with that token. `scripts/api-dev.ts` serves `api/` on the dev server.
- `scripts/build.ts`: the build that deploys. If a change breaks the build, the rest still ships and the broken file stays at its live version.

## Checking a change

`npm install`, then `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build`. Both show real errors.

To see ATV HQ with people in it, open the dev server with `?hq&call=demo` (people dialing in and out), `?hq&call=crowd` (a 48-person call), or `?hq&call=Ana,Ben,Cy` (a fixed list). To try the website mirror, start the dev server with `HQ_PUBLISH_KEY=anything`, POST `{ meeting }` to `/api/hq/token` with an `x-hq-key` header, then POST `{ meeting, people: [{ id, name }] }` to `/api/hq` with the token in `x-hq-token`.

## Phaser gotchas

- Don't name scene fields or methods after Phaser's built-in scene members (`add`, `time`, `input`, `cameras`, `events`, `scale`, `data`, ...). It breaks the scene, and `npm run typecheck` catches it.
- Read keys with Key objects (`this.input.keyboard?.addKey(...)`) or a `window` listener. The scene's `'keydown'` event can fire more than once for one press.
- Key objects miss taps shorter than a frame (`isDown` and `JustDown` both: a key-up clears them). Use a `window` `'keydown'` listener for one-shot presses.
